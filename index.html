About CleanNAIJA

Cleaner Streets. Healthier Communities.
Looking at the state of our environment today, from waste piling up on our streets to neglected public spaces and dirty surroundings, it is clear that something needs to change. Our communities deserve cleaner streets, healthier communities, and a better environment for future generations. That is why Clean Naija is here. Our mission is to make a difference through two essential services. We help tackle public-space cleanliness by allowing people to report dirty public areas and supporting cleanup efforts without collecting a dime for public cleaning. We believe everyone deserves a cleaner environment, regardless of their financial situation. We also provide paid house and compound cleaning services for people who need professional help keeping their homes clean and comfortable. Starting in Lagos and Ogun State, we aim to turn concern into action, one street, one home, and one community at a time. Together, we can take responsibility for our surroundings and build a cleaner future for Nigeria. Clean Naija — Cleaner Streets. Healthier Communities.

Who we are

Born From a Need for Change. Clean Naija was born from a simple but powerful realisation in October 26th, 2026: our communities deserve better. From overflowing refuse dumps in parts of Lagos to blocked drainage channels in areas such as Mushin, Agege, and Ikorodu, we see the challenges that poor waste disposal creates for everyday Nigerians. The foul smells, littered streets, and neglected surroundings are more than an eyesore; they affect the comfort, health, and well-being of our communities. We believe that a cleaner Nigeria is possible, but change begins when people take action. That is why Clean Naija exists: to give people a way to report dirty public spaces and support cleanup efforts without collecting a dime for public cleaning, while also offering paid house and compound cleaning services to those who need them. Starting in Lagos and Ogun State, we want to turn neglected surroundings into cleaner spaces and inspire more Nigerians to take pride in their environment. We are Clean Naija. We believe every street deserves cleanliness, every community deserves care, and every Nigerian deserves a healthier environment.
